Why us

Why Enroll With Us: What 25+ Years of Early Education Looks Like

By the Little Stars early-learning team · October 2, 2026 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • 25+ years of early-childhood experience behind every classroom
  • One standard of quality across more than six schools
  • Hands-on learning, art and play from infants to age 12
  • Two Texas campuses, open Monday to Friday, 6:30 am to 6:00 pm

1Experience you can feel on day one

Choosing a school is a big decision, and the best way to judge a school is by how it feels when you walk in. Our owner has spent more than 25 years in early education and leads more than six schools, and that experience shows up in small, everyday things: how teachers greet a child at the door, how a classroom is arranged, how a hard moment is handled with patience.

Families in The Colony and Lewisville, Texas tell us the same thing during tours: the rooms are calm, the teachers are warm and the children look like they belong. That is not an accident. It is the result of decades of learning what works and refusing to compromise on it.

2A standard of quality that does not change

Quality is easy to promise and hard to deliver every day. Our approach is built on consistency: the same caring teachers, the same predictable routines and the same high expectations in every classroom, from our youngest infants to our school-age children.

Children thrive when they know what to expect. Trust grows from consistency, and trust is what lets a child try something new, ask a question or recover quickly after a difficult day.

Quality is easy to promise and hard to deliver every day.

3Learning by doing, not just sitting

Children learn best through exploration, hands-on experiences and meaningful play. Our programs use imaginative play, art expression, a sensory room and real-world discovery to build the language, focus and confidence children need for kindergarten and beyond.

Our Pre-K and Primary program adds a cultural curriculum that opens children's eyes to the wider world, while our school-age programs help children ages 6 to 12 explore their interests and build meaningful friendships.

4Where a hard week becomes a great one

Every child has tough weeks. What matters is what happens next. Our teachers notice, listen and adjust, working with parents so a child who is struggling one week is thriving the next. Families often tell us the change is the thing they are most grateful for.

If you would like to see it for yourself, book a tour at our The Colony or Lewisville campus. Meet the teachers, walk the classrooms and ask us anything.
