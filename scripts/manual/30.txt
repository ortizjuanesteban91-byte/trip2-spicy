Montaña Redonda + Horseback Riding Combo Miches – Swings & Views
Stand on top of a mountain and breathe in the silence. Ride up to Montaña Redonda by 4x4 truck, swing out over the valley with 360° views of the lagoon and the ocean, then saddle up for a quiet horseback ride through the Miches countryside.
Summit entry and the swings are included; photos at the summit cost extra. Guides lead the ride at a walking pace, so no riding experience is needed.
Round-trip hotel pickup is included from Miches hotels at 7:00 AM or 1:00 PM. Adults only (10+), $105 per person, minimum 2 people per booking. For kids, book the Montaña Redonda Tour Miches. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
Honest note This is one of our high-demand excursions, so on busy days your transport can run 15 to 20 minutes behind schedule because of island traffic and full pickup routes. It doesn't happen every time, but it does happen, and we'd rather tell you now than surprise you. We choose our operators carefully, so when it happens it's a short wait, not a missed tour.
