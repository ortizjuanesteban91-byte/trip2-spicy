Safari Tour Punta Cana – Culture, Countryside & Local Life
Step beyond the resorts and spend a full day in the real Dominican Republic. This Punta Cana safari takes you through the countryside to a country home, a plantation tasting, the Higüey Basilica, a Dominican lunch at the ranch and an hour at Macao Beach.
Taste coffee, cacao and tobacco where they are grown, meet the people who work the land and see how local life runs away from the hotel zone. A local school visit is part of the route when school is in session (not guaranteed).
The day lasts about 8 hours and includes round-trip hotel pickup. Adults (10+) $85, children (4–9) $50, infants (0–3) free. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour.
